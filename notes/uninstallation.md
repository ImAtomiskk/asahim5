# Uninstallation Guide
### Use this if stuck in bootloop or want to return to macOS.
> 🍎 Apple Recovery Mode Instructions
> 1. Make sure that your Mac is turned off. If you can't shut it down normally, press and hold its power button for up to 10 seconds, until your Mac turns off. (Every Mac has a power button. On laptop computers that have Touch ID, press and hold the Touch ID button.)  
> 2. Press and hold the power button again. As you continue to hold it, your Mac turns on and loads startup options. When you see “Loading startup options” or the Options icon, release the power button.  
> 3. Click Options, then click the Continue button that appears beneath it.
> ![](https://cdsassets.apple.com/live/7WUAS350/images/macos/tahoe/macos-tahoe-26-startup-options-recovery-continue.png)

> **Important:** Check the menu bar — the **Tools** menu must contain **Recovery Assistant**.  

>**Click Tools, Then Terminal (or Cmd + Shift + T) and Type**  
> ```csrutil enable```

**Enter Password, When Prompted.**

## Then, Reboot
